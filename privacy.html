Privacy

Suture sends us nothing. Here is what it reads, what it keeps, and when Windows tools it starts use the internet.

In short

  • Suture has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send information about you or your PC to us.
  • Everything it reads and keeps stays on your PC.
  • Some buttons start Windows or Microsoft tools that contact the internet for their own purpose. We do not receive what those tools send.
  • This website has no analytics and sets no cookies.

Who we are

Vincent Khalil Elia El Khoury, Lebanon, suturesupport@gmail.com. For the EU GDPR we are the controller of the little personal data described in sections 5 and 7. The software itself does not process any of it.

1. We do not receive data from the software

Suture runs on your PC. It has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not send information about you or your PC to us. The only data that reaches us from the software is what you choose to send to support (section 7).

2. What the software reads and keeps on your PC

It reads and shows: Windows and hardware settings and identifiers, Microsoft Defender status, startup entries, services, the list of installed apps, drivers, event logs, disk and battery information, the name of the Wi-Fi network, the PC name, your Windows user name, file names, paths, sizes, dates and hashes in the folders it checks, and which pictures carry location or caption tags (Private photos).

It keeps working files on your PC: last readings, logs, undo records, quarantine and settings in its own folder; the licence and trial records in your user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA% and %APPDATA%, plus a PC-wide copy of the trial record in %ProgramData%); downloaded installers in %TEMP%; registry backups; and backups in C:\SutureVault if you set them up. These files can contain any of the items above. Suture does not upload them. The in-app switch "Hide personal details" blurs the Wi-Fi name and PC name on screen. You can delete the files: the uninstaller asks whether to keep them.

The Insights checks (Pro) keep a few files of their own under logs\insights\<check>\ in Suture's folder: the old values of the Windows settings you switch in Windows surprises, so Put back can restore them, and the saved result of a "Read full health" disk read. Suture reads the recovery key only if you ask, after a Windows administrator prompt. It is sealed for your Windows account in a temporary file in your own folder for a few seconds, then read into memory and the file is deleted at once. It stays in memory for 3 minutes at most and is never written to logs or settings. "Save a copy" and the Health report write a file only where you choose in the Windows Save window. The Health report is built from readings already on your PC, removes personal details such as the PC name, user name, serial numbers, network addresses, Wi-Fi names, e-mail addresses and folder paths in your profile by default, and is never uploaded. Please read it before you send it to anyone. The Insights checks send nothing to us or to anyone else.

3. Licence key

Your key is stored on your PC and checked there against a public key inside the software. There is no activation server. The key contains an order reference (a hash), not your name or email.

4. When the software uses the network

Only when you open a page or press a button, or as steps of Smart Scan and Full audit: App updates (Microsoft's winget contacts Microsoft's package sources; Suture downloads installers from the app makers' own sites); the Network page and the failure-analysis connection test (pings and connections to 1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8, your router and a Microsoft test name); Windows Update (Windows' own service, which receives the client name "Suture"); Defender definition update (Microsoft); deep crash analysis, only after you confirm (Microsoft's public symbol server, which receives the name, version and identifier of every program and driver module in the crash dump; the dump itself is not uploaded).

These contacts are made by Windows or Microsoft's tools, or are plain downloads, and are covered by Microsoft's or the app maker's terms and privacy statement. The software lists every case on its Network disclosure page. A background setting that reads the Network and App updates pages before you open them is off unless you switch it on.

5. This website and orders

This website has no analytics, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep its own server logs of visits under its own privacy statement.

When you buy, the checkout is run by Dodo Payments, which collects your payment and contact details under its own privacy policy and sends us your name, email address, country, order number and the amount paid. We use them to send your licence key, give support, keep accounting records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and handle refunds. Legal bases: contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest in support. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

6. Components

The Edge WebView2 runtime (Microsoft, part of Windows) draws the window; if unavailable Suture opens a Microsoft Edge app window. Node.js and WebView2 DLLs are shipped under their own licences. They are not used to collect data.

7. Support data you send us

If you write to support or send the support information, a support pack or a crash dump, we receive what you send, for the purpose of answering you. The support information and pack have your user name and PC name replaced where the software can. The optional crash dump file is a slice of memory and can contain text, passwords or keys from programs that were open, so the software warns you before you include it. We keep support emails for 2 years and delete attachments after 30 days. Legal basis: answering your request (legitimate interest) and the contract. To have your data deleted, email us.

We keep licence and order records (order reference, key reference, date, email address) for as long as tax and accounting law requires to deliver keys, resend a lost key to the address on the order, handle refunds and keep accounts.

8. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection for the data in sections 5 and 7; complaint to your supervisory authority in your country of residence.

9. Children, changes, contact

Not directed at children. The date below changes when this page does; material changes are announced here. Contact: suturesupport@gmail.com, Lebanon.

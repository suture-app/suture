What Suture does differently

Reads first, asks before it acts

Smart Scan only reads. Nothing changes until you pick a fix, read what it will do, and confirm. No "fix everything" surprises.

Undo for most changes

Most fixes can be undone in one click, and Suture can make a restore point first.

Plain words, no scare tactics

Each finding is explained in everyday language. No red "thousands of problems" counters and no speed or security promises.

Everything it can change is public

A public page, "What Suture changes", lists every change the app can make, before you install it.

No account, no telemetry, no ads

The scan runs on your PC, and its logs and undo records stay there. The licence key is checked on your PC, with no licence server.

More than cleaning

Crash and failure analysis, Defender status, backup checks (Backup guardian), startup and services, app and Windows updates, and Windows Update repair, in one place.

A free edition that is really useful

The scan, its findings and all the safety tools stay free with no time limit. Each Pro fix also shows the steps to do it yourself in Windows.

Paid once, not every year

One payment covers all 1.x updates. Same key after a reinstall, a Windows reset or a new PC.
